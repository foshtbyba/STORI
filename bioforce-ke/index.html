  • Sharp pain in the knees when going up or down stairs — or with every step;
  • Morning stiffness — the body feels "frozen" and does not move for 30–60 minutes after waking up;
  • Chronic back pain — cannot bend down, cannot get out of bed;
  • Swollen knees, ankles or fingers;
  • Cracking and clicking sounds in the joints with every movement;
